## 描述（Description）

Can you tell which AI video is fake? A study of 1,043 people found that most of us are barely better than a coin flip. This video explains why every "how to spot AI video" checklist is going out of date, and what still works.

0:00 The quiz
1:27 How a video is made
2:54 Why the old tells expire
4:08 The second study: looking longer

6:48 What to do

Sources named in the video: Runway's "The Turing Reel" study; UC Berkeley's perceptual study of AI video authenticity (Davodi, Barrington, Farid, Cooper); Ho, Jain and Abbeel's 2020 diffusion paper; Google's SynthID Detector; C2PA Content Credentials.

Every moving image in this video is an animated illustration. No AI-generated video or footage is used.
