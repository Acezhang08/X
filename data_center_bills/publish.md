## 描述（直接粘贴；不含任何链接，来源只写名字）
Data centers made electricity cheaper — until the grid ran out of room. In PJM, America's biggest power grid, one price jumped from $29 to $329 in two years, and this video follows the money from the auction to your electric bill.

You'll see how a capacity auction works, what a power-industry research institute found about data centers and electricity rates, why a market monitor says data centers are the primary reason prices spiked, and which rules states are changing to decide who pays.
