**Q1: Jan Ernst Matzeliger**
- Born Sept. 15, 1852, in Paramaribo, Dutch Guiana (now Suriname), to a Dutch engineer father and an enslaved Surinamese mother.
- Emigrated to the U.S. in 1876 and settled in Lynn, Massachusetts, in 1877. He worked in shoe factories.
- Patented his shoe lasting machine on March 20, 1883 (U.S. Patent No. 274,207).

- Died of tuberculosis on Aug. 24, 1889, at age 36.
